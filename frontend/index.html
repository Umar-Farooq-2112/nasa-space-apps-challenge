How to read this
  • Flipbook: each frame is a different date (shown under the picture). Stars stay in place; something that drifts is moving across the sky.
  • Blue circle + trail: Mover Spotter found a source whose position changes steadily over time. The label is its speed in arcseconds per year. It must appear in most frames, so one-off specks are ignored.
  • Click a circle to turn it yellow and see its brightness over time.
  • Time-colour: every date gets a colour, all frames are stacked. Fixed stars come out white. A mover leaves a rainbow trail, blue at its old positions and red at its newest.
  • Difference: newest third minus oldest third. Fixed stars cancel. A mover is a blue spot where it was and a red spot where it is now.
  • Sensitivity: lower finds fainter movers but also more false alarms.
  • Band: use one band at a time. Different bands show different stars, so mixing them looks like movement.
